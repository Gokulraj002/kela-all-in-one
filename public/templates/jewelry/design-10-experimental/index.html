The Atelier

Darkness is a material too.

We work the way cinematographers light a scene — every Kela Jewels piece is modeled against shadow before it is cast. Blackened gold, smoked settings, stones chosen for how they behave in low light.

    The Maison

    Founded in a darkroom, not a boardroom.

    Kela Jewels began when a photographer and a goldsmith started arguing about light. Seven years later, the maison still prototypes every piece under a single spotlight in a blacked-out studio in Mumbai — because a jewel is only finished when it survives the dark.
