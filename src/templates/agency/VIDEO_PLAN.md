# ATELIER · Creative Agency — VIDEO_PLAN.md

One signature cinematic clip per design (~10s, seamless-loop feel, H.264 720p).
File: `assets/hero-loop.mp4` in each design folder. Poster: `assets/hero.jpg`.

## Integration rules (binding, from coffee)
- Shared `<LoopVideo src poster alt className>` from `../../../_shared` — do NOT hand-roll.
- `<video muted autoplay loop playsinline preload="metadata" poster>`; pause offscreen
  (IntersectionObserver 0.15); reduced-motion → poster only; error → poster remains.
- Photorealistic, on-palette grade, no text, no watermarks, no visible faces
  (hands/process OK; 09's portrait is a still, not the video).
- Studio life, process macros, kinetic light — cinematic, never corporate stock.

## Storyboards

### 01 · design-01-portfolio — "The pin-up" (10s) — HERO background
- 0–3s: slow dolly-in on a studio wall dense with pinned campaign prints, daylight.
- 3–7s: drift continues across the wall; a hand enters frame, straightens one print.
- 7–10s: settle on a hero print; loop on the ambient drift.
- Grade: paper white / ink / signal-red accents in the prints.

### 02 · design-02-brand — "Letterforms" (10s) — HERO background
- 0–3s: extreme macro of a nib sketching a letterform, ink pooling.
- 3–7s: pull-back across a desk of logo iterations, tracing paper layers.
- 7–10s: rest on the chosen mark circled in ochre; loop on paper stillness.
- Grade: bone / espresso / ochre.

### 03 · design-03-motion — "The beam" (10s) — HERO background

- 3–7s: slow push through the haze toward a glowing screen, amber flares.
- 7–10s: hold on the light field; loop on the haze drift.
- Grade: near-black / amber.
