# ATELIER — Creative Agency: Art Direction

Binding: `REACT_TEMPLATE_CONTRACT.md` (tokens), `AGENCY_BRIEF.md` (directions).
Global rules: no generic gradients, no neon, no emojis; each palette 60/30/10;
10 unique display+body pairings (no repeats); metallics only as 10% accents.

Contract tokens: `--color-background --color-surface --color-primary --color-secondary
--color-accent --color-text --color-muted --font-display --font-body`.

---

## 01 — Studio Meridian · Portfolio-first
**Palette** 60/30/10 paper / ink / signal red
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| background | `#F7F5F0` | Paper ground |
| surface | `#ECE8DE` | Case card surface |
| primary | `#16130E` | Ink headlines, nav |
| secondary | `#5C5546` | Rules, labels |
| accent | `#E03E2D` | Signal red — CTAs, active filter, outcome numerals |
| text | `#1C1812` | Body |
| muted | `#8B8474` | Captions |
**Type:** Archivo (700–900, `font-stretch` expanded for headlines) + Inter.
Archivo black at clamp(3–6rem), tight leading, uppercase for case titles; Inter for
everything else; outcome metrics in Archivo 800, accent red.
**Layout:** Proof-dense work grid; filter chips; hero is the latest case full-bleed with
a red outcome numeral ("+212%").
**Imagery grade:** Daylight studio, true paper whites, red appears in the work itself.
**Personality:** Confident · Direct · Commercial.

## Cross-design contrast (for QA)
- Light grounds: 01, 02, 04, 05, 06, 08, 09. Dark grounds: 03, 07, 10.
- Accent hues all distinct in role: signal red / ochre / amber / cobalt /
  international orange / acid yellow / warm silver-gold / electric blue /
  terracotta / lime.
- No two designs share a display+body pairing (10 unique pairings above).
- Fraunces-free category — deliberate, to prove range beyond the coffee set.
