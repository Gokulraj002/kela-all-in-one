# ATELIER · Real Estate — Art Direction

Palettes are binding per design. Photography must feel genuinely premium:
golden-hour or blue-hour exteriors, considered interiors with real light,
no fisheye distortion look, no plastic 3D renders, no text or watermarks.

## Per-design direction

### 01 · design-01-villas — "Meridian Estates"
- Palette: deep teal `#0E2A30` / champagne `#C9A961` / warm ivory `#F4EFE6`
- Type: Cormorant Garamond + Manrope. Whispered eyebrows, huge display.
- Imagery: dusk exteriors, glowing glass pavilions, still water, stone.
- Mood: exclusive, cinematic, quiet.

### 02 · design-02-urban — "Stack & Stone"
- Palette: ink `#141414` / bone `#EDEAE2` / signal orange `#E4572E`
- Type: Archivo (700/800, tight) + Inter. Tabular numerals for data.
- Imagery: blue-hour towers, crisp balconies, efficient interiors.
- Mood: sharp, fast, no-nonsense.

### 03 · design-03-commercial — "Northgate Works"
- Palette: navy `#1B2A4A` / graphite `#2B2F36` / paper `#F5F3EE` / brass `#A9884B`
- Type: IBM Plex Serif + IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono (specs).
- Imagery: atriums, floor plates, facade grids, empty volumes with light.
- Mood: confident, precise, institutional.

### 04 · design-04-plots — "Aaranya Acres"
- Palette: parchment `#EFE6D4` / forest `#2E4A34` / earth `#8A6D4B` / ink `#211C13`
- Type: Fraunces + Instrument Sans. Survey-map hairlines, plot-number ticks.
- Imagery: golden-hour aerials, avenue trees, lake edge, soil and grass detail.
- Mood: rooted, generous, legacy.

### 05 · design-05-heritage — "The Lime & Lintel Co."
- Palette: aged stone `#CBBFA8` / oxblood `#5A2320` / limewash `#F1EAD9` / brass `#A9884B`
- Type: Playfair Display + Manrope. Letterpress-feel rules and seals.
- Imagery: carved stone, colonnades, craftsman hands, lime mortar, brass.
- Mood: storied, tactile, reverent.

### 06 · design-06-coliving — "Kindred House"
- Palette: cream `#FAF5EA` / terracotta `#C96F3F` / leaf `#5A7A4E` / ink `#26221B`
- Type: DM Serif Display + Plus Jakarta Sans. Rounded warmth, sticker-like badges.
- Imagery: sunlit courtyards, long tables, hammocks, plants, shared kitchens.
- Mood: warm, social, alive.

### 07 · design-07-smart — "Halcyon Living"
- Palette: near-black `#101114` / amber `#E8A33D` / dawn blue `#7A93B5` / oak `#B08A5A`
- Type: Space Grotesk + Inter. Fine hairlines, glowing accents.
- Imagery: minimal interiors at dawn/dusk, light coves, quiet tech details.
- Mood: calm, intelligent, nocturnal.

### 08 · design-08-retreat — "Stillwater Reserve"
- Palette: warm white `#F7F4EC` / aqua `#5E8B8C` / driftwood `#A08B6D` / deep sea `#173038`
- Type: Cormorant Garamond + Instrument Sans. Vast whitespace, slow rhythm.
- Imagery: infinity pools, ocean horizons, palms, bleached wood, linen.
- Mood: slow, breathable, far away.

### 09 · design-09-trust — "Sahaj Homes"
- Palette: honest white `#FBFAF7` / brick `#B4552D` / leaf `#5A7A4E` / ink `#2A2620`
- Type: Source Serif 4 + Public Sans. Clear hierarchy, generous line-height.
- Imagery: bright real apartments, construction progress, community spaces,
  sunlit rooms. Honest, never glossy.
- Mood: warm, clear, dependable.

### 10 · design-10-archviz — "Studio Monolith"
- Palette: concrete `#8E8C86` / black `#0C0C0C` / bone `#EDEAE2` / persimmon `#F4562A`
- Type: Archivo Expanded feel via Archivo + Space Grotesk. Kinetic headlines.
- Imagery: graphic concrete forms, hard light, dramatic shadow, massing models.
- Mood: daring, graphic, sculptural.
