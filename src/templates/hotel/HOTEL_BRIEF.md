# ATELIER — Hotel & Resort Category: Industry Research Brief

**Purpose:** Ground the 10 hotel designs in researched hospitality conventions, then define a UNIQUE direction for each. Sources: luxury hotel UX studies, booking-funnel research, resort storytelling patterns, wellness-brand design language.

**Standing truths for the category:**
- Hotel sites convert **bookings**: dates, rates, availability, and location must be reachable within seconds. Every design needs a persistent booking affordance (sticky bar, nav CTA, or hero widget) — never buried.
- Trust currency: real photography, specific details (room sizes in sq ft/m, named restaurants, check-in times), guest reviews with names, awards. Vague luxury copy converts nothing.
- Mobile is the majority of discovery: tap-to-call, maps links, and a thumb-reachable "Check availability" must work at 360px.
- Never hide the price behind a contact form. Never use a fake booking engine that goes nowhere — the booking widget is a realistic date/guest selector that leads to a clear "Reserve" step (demo).
- Hospitality imagery must feel genuinely luxurious: golden-hour light, real water, honest materials (stone, teak, linen, brass). No plastic renders.

**Interaction inventory (hotel-specific, max two per design):** booking widget (dates/guests), room selector with rates, scroll-driven room gallery, experience timeline, spa treatment menu, dining reservation teaser, estate/property map, testimonial wall, season/weather note, concierge chat teaser. Each design uses at most two.

## design-01-palace — Luxury Palace Resort
- **Working name:** The Rajwada Palace
- **Audience:** 35–65, luxury travelers, celebration bookers (weddings, anniversaries), international tourists.
- **Conversion goal:** Direct booking of suites + wedding/event inquiry.
- **Section order:** 1. Hero — cinematic palace façade at golden hour, "A palace, not a hotel." 2. Booking bar — dates/guests sticky. 3. The legend — palace history in 3 beats. 4. Suites — 4 signature suites with rates. 5. Dining — 3 restaurants, chef notes. 6. Experiences — royal rituals (private dinner, vintage car, spa). 7. Weddings & events teaser. 8. Testimonials. 9. Location/practical. 10. Footer.
- **Nav pattern:** Regal centered wordmark with crest-like monogram, serif links, "Reserve" gold CTA. Mobile: sticky bottom booking bar.
- **Key interactions:** Curtain-reveal room gallery (signature); booking widget with live rate math.
- **Image shot-list:** 1. Hero — palace façade golden hour with fountain. 2. Suite — royal suite bedroom, four-poster. 3. Suite — marble bathroom with brass. 4. Dining — candlelit durbar hall restaurant. 5. Detail — carved arch / jharokha light play.
- **Differentiation:** Regal cinematic maximalism — the grandest design in the category. Serif display, gold on deep maroon/ink, ceremonial motion.
