# ATELIER · Hotel & Resort — VIDEO_PLAN.md

One signature cinematic clip per design (6–10s, seamless-loop feel).
File: `assets/hero-loop.mp4` in each design folder. Poster: `assets/hero.jpg`.

## Integration rules (binding — same as coffee)

- Use the shared `<LoopVideo src poster alt className>` from `../../_shared`. Do NOT hand-roll.
- `<video muted autoplay loop playsinline preload="metadata" poster>`; pause offscreen (IO, threshold 0.15); `prefers-reduced-motion` → poster only; on error → poster remains.
- Photorealistic, on-palette grade per design, no text, no watermarks, no visible faces. If cheap/plasticky/off-palette → regenerate.

## Storyboards

### 01 · design-01-palace — "Golden hour at the palace" (9s) — HERO background
- 0–3s: wide on palace façade, fountain in foreground, warm haze.
- 3–6s: imperceptible slow push-in; fountain water catching light, birds crossing.
- 6–9s: settle; loop on water shimmer. Grade: deep maroon/gold, cinematic warmth.

### 02 · design-02-beach — "First tide" (9s) — HERO background
- 0–3s: pale dawn beach, gentle wave edge approaching.
- 3–6s: wave washes over sand toward camera, foam dissolving.
- 6–9s: water recedes, wet sand gleaming; loop on the wash. Grade: desaturated pale, airy.

### 03 · design-03-urban — "Blue hour rooftop" (8s) — HERO background
- 0–3s: rooftop terrace, city lights flickering on at dusk.
- 3–6s: slow lateral drift across the skyline; a cocktail glass catches light in foreground.
- 6–8s: settle; loop on light shimmer. Grade: high-contrast ink/amber.

### 04 · design-04-lodge — "Fireside" (8s) — HERO background
- 0–3s: stone fireplace, flames rising, warm glow on timber.
- 3–6s: embers pulse; snow visible falling past the window behind.
- 6–8s: flame settle; loop on ember glow. Grade: warm amber/charcoal.

### 05 · design-05-haveli — "Courtyard light" (9s) — HERO background

### 06 · design-06-eco — "Canopy mist" (10s) — HERO background

### 08 · design-08-spa — "Still water" (10s) — HERO background

### 09 · design-09-villas — "Estate at sunset" (9s) — HERO background
