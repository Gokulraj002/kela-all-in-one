# ATELIER · Restaurant — Category Brief

10 restaurant website experiences. Each must feel like a different restaurant
group hired a different studio. Food photography is the make-or-break:
every dish must look genuinely delicious — Michelin-grade styling, honest
texture, no plasticky renders. If a food image looks cheap, regenerate it.

## The 10 directions

| # | ID | Name | Concept |
|---|----|------|---------|
| 01 | design-01-finedining | Lumière | Fine dining tasting menu, hushed luxury. 7-course tasting as the page spine. |
| 02 | design-02-trattoria | Cucina Terra | Rustic regional kitchen, warm and hearty. Family-table energy, chalkboard soul. |
| 03 | design-03-modernindian | Agni | Modern Indian, refined and bold. Fire as the design metaphor. |
| 04 | design-04-street | Chowk | Street food casual, loud and craveable. Maximum appetite, minimum ceremony. |
| 05 | design-05-omakase | Umi | Omakase counter, Japanese restraint. 12 seats, one chef, no noise. |
| 06 | design-06-farm | Soil & Stem | Farm-to-table, honest and green. The menu follows the seasons. |
| 07 | design-07-rooftop | Aurelia | Rooftop lounge & bar, golden-hour glamour. Cocktails at altitude. |
| 08 | design-08-patisserie | Butter & Bloom | Bakery & patisserie, delicate craft. Morning light and laminated dough. |
| 09 | design-09-cloud | Hotbox | Cloud kitchen, delivery-first, fast. Built for thumbs and 30-minute promises. |
| 10 | design-10-chefstable | Encore | Experimental chef's table, theatrical. Dinner as a three-act performance. |

## Global constraints

- Contract: `~/workspace/atelier/REACT_TEMPLATE_CONTRACT.md` (binding).
- Conventions: `BUILDER_GUIDE.md` in this folder (adapted from coffee).
- Motion: `MOTION.md` — one signature motion per design + house grammar.
- Video: `VIDEO_PLAN.md` — storyboard FIRST, then generate to plan.
- 5 unique images per design, never reused across designs. No text/watermarks.
  Food must be photorealistic and appetizing — this category lives or dies on it.
- Prices in ₹ (numbers, rendered via `price()`).
- No lorem ipsum, no emojis, no generic gradients, no neon.

## Per-design section order & differentiation

### 01 Lumière — fine dining tasting menu
Sections: nav (minimal, wordmark + Reserve) → hero (dark, single plated dish,
tasting-menu promise) → philosophy (chef's note) → **the tasting** (7 courses,
scroll mechanic #1) → wine pairing note → private dining → visit/reserve →
footer (quiet, colophon-like). CTA: "Reserve a table" — singular, unhurried.
Product presentation: courses as numbered movements (I–VII), not a menu list.

### 02 Cucina Terra — rustic trattoria
Sections: nav (warm, wood-toned, phone number prominent) → hero (family table,
steam, abundance) → our story (nonna narrative) → **the kitchen's dishes**
(scroll mechanic #2) → handmade pasta feature → Sunday table (family-style
offering) → visit → footer (warm, hand-drawn feel). CTA: "Book the big table".
Product presentation: dishes as family recipes with origin notes ("Nonna Elba's").

### 03 Agni — modern Indian
Sections: nav (bold, dark with fire accent) → hero (fire + plated modern Indian
dish) → philosophy (fire as ingredient) → **the menu** (scroll mechanic #3) →
tasting journeys (2 set menus) → the bar (Indian-spirited cocktails) →
reserve → footer. CTA: "Reserve". Product presentation: dishes grouped by
element (Smoke / Earth / Fire / Ice).

### 04 Chowk — street food
Sections: nav (loud, order-now pill) → hero (sizzling street cart, kinetic type)
→ **the cravings wall** (scroll mechanic #4) → legends (iconic dishes with
stories) → find the cart (locations) → order → footer (loud, sticker-like).
CTA: "Order now" — highest density. Product presentation: crave-cards with
spice-level meters and "legend" badges.

### 05 Umi — omakase
Sections: nav (whisper-quiet, 12 seats note) → hero (single nigiri, vast
whitespace) → the counter (chef intro) → **the progression** (scroll
mechanic #5) → the rules (omakase etiquette, beautifully set) → reserve
(calendar-like) → footer (minimal). CTA: "Request a seat" — scarcity-framed.
Product presentation: courses as a served progression, not a choosable menu.

### 06 Soil & Stem — farm-to-table
Sections: nav (green, honest) → hero (morning harvest, hands in soil) →
our farm (growers) → **the seasons** (scroll mechanic #6) → today's harvest
menu → supper club → visit → footer. CTA: "See today's menu".
Product presentation: menu organized by season dial; dishes carry farm-source
notes ("from Row 7, picked this morning").

### 07 Aurelia — rooftop lounge
Sections: nav (glamorous, golden) → hero (cocktail at golden hour, skyline) →
the hour (golden-hour ritual) → **sips & plates** (scroll mechanic #7) →
the view (skyline feature) → private skies (events) → reserve → footer.
CTA: "Reserve golden hour". Product presentation: cocktails as the stars,
small plates as supporting cast.

### 08 Butter & Bloom — patisserie
Sections: nav (delicate, morning-fresh) → hero (laminated croissant, morning
light) → the craft (lamination story) → **the case** (scroll mechanic #8) →
morning bakes schedule (what comes out of the oven when) → custom cakes →
visit → footer. CTA: "Pre-order for morning".
Product presentation: the glass case — viennoiserie, entremets, breads —
with bake-time notes ("out of the oven 7:40").

### 09 Hotbox — cloud kitchen
Sections: nav (app-like, track-order) → hero (bold, steam bursting from a box)
→ **the lineup** (scroll mechanic #9) → how it works (3 steps) → combos &
deals → coverage (delivery zones) → order → footer (functional). CTA:
"Order now — 30 min". Product presentation: dispatch lanes by craving
(Biryani lane / Burger lane / Dessert lane) with live-feel timers.

### 10 Encore — chef's table
Sections: nav (theatrical, act-based) → hero (cloche, smoke, drama) →
the premise (dinner as theatre) → **the three acts** (scroll mechanic #10) →
the chef (director's note) → cast (team as cast list) → tickets (booking as
box office) → footer (playbill). CTA: "Book tickets".
Product presentation: menu as a playbill — Act I/II/III with scene-like
course names.
