# ATELIER · E-commerce — Art Direction

## 01 · design-01-flagship — "Atelier Flagship"
Museum retail. Bone `#F4F1EA` ground, ink `#1A1815` text, hairline rules,
single accent of deep bronze `#8C6A3F`. Cormorant Garamond + Inter.
Vast whitespace, one product per viewport, small-caps eyebrows, numbered
pieces ("Nº 01 — The Sculpted Tote"). Imagery: gallery interior, pedestal
product shots, macro leather grain, packaging ritual.

## 02 · design-02-bazaar — "The Grand Bazaar"
Joyful density. Warm paper `#FBF3E4`, ink `#221A12`, saffron `#E8862E`,
deep teal `#0F5E5B`, chili `#C63B22`. Archivo (700/900) + Work Sans.
Tilted stall cards, sticker badges, hand-drawn dividers (SVG, not emoji).
Imagery: market stalls, textile stacks, brass goods, hands exchanging,
marigold/lantern color.

## 03 · design-03-maison — "Maison Éclat"
Dark editorial luxury. Near-black `#0E0D0B`, champagne `#D9C39A`,
smoke `#8A857C`. Cormorant Garamond italic + Manrope. Letterbox bars,
full-bleed spreads, slow crossfades, generous leading. Imagery: boutique
interior at night, silk drape, watch/jewel macro, leather goods.

## 04 · design-04-drop — "DROP/04"
Street urgency. Bone `#EFEDE6`, ink `#111110`, volt orange `#FF4D00`
(never neon glow — flat). Space Grotesk + Inter. Oversized numerals,
ticker tape, hard rules, stamp badges. Imagery: sneaker macro, streetwear
flat-lay, box stack, urban wall, unboxing hands.

## 05 · design-05-eco — "Terra & Thread"
Honest earth. Cream `#F6F1E7`, moss `#4A5D3A`, clay `#B0713E`,
charcoal text `#2A2620`. Fraunces + Manrope. Organic dividers, seed-paper
textures (CSS grain, subtle), impact counters. Imagery: kraft packaging,
hands planting, natural fibers, refill station, sunlit workshop.

## 06 · design-06-tech — "VOLT/STATE"
Dark precision. Graphite `#101216`, steel `#9AA3B2`, signal cyan `#57D0E8`
(flat, thin usage), white text. Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Mono. HUD
annotations, crosshairs, spec tables, hairline grids. Imagery: gadget
macro, circuit detail, headphones studio shot, desk setup, dark product
turntable.

## 07 · design-07-apparel — "Atelier Vestiaire"
Editorial fashion. Warm gray `#EDE8DF`, ink `#1C1A17`, oxblood `#6E2A2A`.
Bodoni Moda + Inter. Full-bleed lookbook pages, folio numbers, pull
quotes, product tags on looks. Imagery: lookbook portraits (partial
faces), fabric macro, atelier rack, tailoring detail, accessories.

## 08 · design-08-home — "Hearth & Haven"
Warm domestic. Linen `#F3EDE2`, clay `#A96B47`, oak `#7A5C3E`,
ink `#241F19`. Fraunces + Manrope. Room scenes with shoppable hotspots,
soft shadows, material swatches. Imagery: styled living room, bedroom
vignette, kitchen detail, ceramics, textile close-up.

## 09 · design-09-beauty — "PÉTALE"
Soft science. Blush `#F7ECE6`, sand `#E9DCCB`, rosewood `#8A4B3C`,
ink `#2B2320`. Cormorant Garamond + Jost. Dissolving layers, ingredient
callouts, airy grids, dewy macro. Imagery: serum bottle, cream swirl,
botanical ingredients, shelf still-life, texture macro.

## 10 · design-10-brutal — "RAW/STOCK"
No decoration. Concrete `#D8D5CE`, black `#0A0A0A`, safety yellow
`#F5C518` (flat). Archivo + Space Mono. Raw table, thick rules,
uppercase, visible grid. Imagery: warehouse aisle, parcel stack, barcode
macro, concrete wall, product on concrete.

## Global image rules
Photorealistic, on-palette grade, no text/watermarks. If a generated
image looks plasticky, off-palette, or cheap → regenerate. Never reuse an
image across designs.
