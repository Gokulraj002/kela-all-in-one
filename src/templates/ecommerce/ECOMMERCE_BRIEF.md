# ATELIER · E-commerce Category — Brief

10 premium e-commerce website experiences. Every design sells real-feeling
products with INR pricing, working cart affordances (demo), and a distinct
art direction. No design may look or feel like another.

## Category-wide rules

- **Real commerce bones**: nav with cart affordance, hero, product lineup
  (4+ products with names + INR prices via `price()`), at least one
  conversion section (reviews / shipping / guarantee), footer.
- **Demo cart**: a cart drawer or count that responds to "Add" clicks is
  required on every design (no checkout — demo only, label it honestly).
- **Copy**: real-feeling product names, INR prices (numbers in content.js),
  no lorem ipsum, no emojis, no placeholder text.
- **Images**: 5 unique premium JPGs per design, art-directed to the design's
  palette. No repeats across designs. Photorealistic. No text, no
  watermarks. Faces only where the design demands (apparel lookbook may show
  partial faces, never stock-smile).
- **Video**: one ~10s signature loop per design, storyboarded in
  VIDEO_PLAN.md first, then generated to plan.
- **Scroll product flow**: each design gets ONE of the 10 mechanics in
  MOTION.md — no repeats, none borrowed from coffee's set.
- **Spacing**: creative-director whitespace pass before QA. E-commerce
  density is a choice (bazaar, brutal), never an accident.
- **Accessibility**: keyboard-reachable CTAs, alt text, focus styles,
  `prefers-reduced-motion` path that shows everything statically.
